# 00. Master Prompt: A Product for Waldorf-Inspired education approach in Vietnam

## 1. What Do I Want to Build, and Why? (Intent & Outcome)
- The product must include XuVi Story: a feature that helps parents practice telling folktales (upload a story, divide it into memorable scenes, generate age-appropriate watercolor or crayon illustrations for each scene, record a retelling, and provide comparative feedback).
- It should be able to generate the following documents as needed:
| No. | Document                                              | Minimum required content                                                                                                                            |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Overall education program                            | Educational philosophy, goals, qualities and competencies to develop, learning areas, teaching methods, instructional hours, and assessment principles |
| 2   | Detailed education program by grade/age              | List of subjects, objectives for each subject, learning outcomes, instructional content, instructional hours, teaching methods, and learning materials |
| 3   | Alignment matrix with the Ministry of Education and Training's General Education Curriculum | Each required outcome in the official curriculum, corresponding Waldorf-Inspired education approach content, time allocated, verification method, and degree of alignment |
| 4   | Academic-year education plan                         | Academic-year dates, number of school weeks, subject distribution, monthly themes/projects, experiential activities, assessment schedule, and contingency plan |
| 5   | Timetable and weekly lesson plan                      | Schedule from 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., Monday through Friday; content of each session, responsible person, objectives and actual duration; distinguish instructional time from routines and activities |
| 6   | Teaching journal and student assessment records       | Teaching dates, content delivered, attendance, assessment results, assignments, learning artifacts, progress notes, and periodic reports |

## 2. Hard Constraints & Non-Goals
- Choose the tech stack and decide which Meta products SHOULD be used (Wonder, Spark, Image, Voice Transcribe, SAM 3.1, Spark Open, Glimmer, Baseten, Modal, Fireworks). You are not required to use all of them; explain your choices in the research plan.
